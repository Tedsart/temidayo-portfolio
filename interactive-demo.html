02 — WHAT I DO

Three things, done properly.

01

Analyze

Turn raw data into useful evidence through statistical thinking, exploration and analysis.

  • Exploratory analysis
  • Data quality & cleaning
  • Statistical testing

02

Visualize

Build dashboards and visualizations that reveal patterns instead of simply displaying numbers.

  • Dashboard design
  • Visual hierarchy
  • Interaction & filters

03

Communicate

Turn findings into clear stories, reports and presentations people can understand.

  • Narrative structure
  • Executive reporting
  • Presentation design

03 — HOW I THINK

Understand → Explore → Analyze → Visualize → Communicate

01

Understand

Start with the question, not the data. Who is asking, what decision hangs on the answer.

02

Explore

Profile the dataset. Check quality, distributions, gaps and outliers before any number is trusted.

03

Analyze

Test the relationships that matter. Keep the method simple enough to defend.

04

Visualize

Design charts that reveal a pattern at a glance, readable on any screen.

05

Communicate

Close with the finding, the evidence and the recommendation.
